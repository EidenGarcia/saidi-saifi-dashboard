Definitions

SAIDI — System Average Interruption Duration Index: total customer-minutes of sustained interruption divided by customers served. Average minutes each customer was without power.

SAIFI — System Average Interruption Frequency Index: customers interrupted divided by customers served. Average number of sustained outages per customer.

CAIDI — SAIDI ÷ SAIFI: average minutes to restore an interrupted customer.

OMA baseline and benchmark — The baseline is the minimum performance the Energy Bureau measures LUMA against under its Operation and Maintenance Agreement (OMA): SAIDI 1,243 minutes and SAIFI 10.6 interruptions per customer per year. The benchmark is the long-term goal based on comparable U.S. utilities: SAIDI 102 minutes and SAIFI 1.0. Lower is better, and both apply to system-wide permanent T&D figures, so the page shows them only for that view.

Permanent and temporary — Permanent (sustained) interruptions last more than 5 minutes; they are what SAIDI, SAIFI and CAIDI count under IEEE 1366, and the workbook reports them directly. Temporary (momentary) interruptions last 5 minutes or less; their frequency is MAIFI. Temporary figures are rebuilt from the outage log, and All adds the two together.

Part of the grid — Distribution covers primary and feeder outages; transmission & substation covers outages recorded as substation events. Together they make the T&D total.

Regions — The seven operating regions group the 16 districts by the region code at the start of each outage's event number. A region's figures weight its districts by customers served, which is worked out from the outage log and the workbook's district figures.

All figures are sustained outages calculated under the IEEE 1366 standard, with major event days (TMED) excluded so storms such as hurricanes do not distort normal performance, and fiscal years run July to June. System and district figures are the workbook's own reported values; region figures, the cause breakdown and major-event amounts are built from them and from the outage-log sheets, and match the reported totals from FY2023 on. Toa Baja is reported as a district from FY2025, so the Bayamón region covers Bayamón alone before then.
